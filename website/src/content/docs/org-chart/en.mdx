## Overview

The interactive org chart is the primary way to visualize your organizational structure. It displays roles as nested containers, where a role that contains sub-roles represents a team or department, or as a classic top-down tree if you prefer that reading. You can:

- **Click** a role to zoom in and see its sub-roles.
- **Drag** to pan around the org chart.
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out.
- Switch between views using the view selector.
- Use the **minimap** shown in the bottom right corner on desktop, which tells you where you are as soon as the org chart grows past the screen, and click or drag it to move around.
- **Right click** a role, a member or the background to open the menu of actions available there (see below).

## Right click menu

A right click opens the actions available where you clicked, without leaving the org chart:

- On a **role**, the actions of its panel: edit it, move it into another role, duplicate it, export it, turn it into a base role or separate it from one, and archive it. Moving and duplicating open a form where you pick the target role.
- On a **member**, remove them from the role you clicked them in.
- On the **background**, the org chart options: keyboard shortcuts, base roles, vacant roles, history and sharing.

Each menu only lists what your [governance mode](/en/docs/governance-modes) and your rights allow.

## Views

The org chart offers three views, each of which can be folded around the selected role. You can set the default view in the [organization settings](/en/docs/organizations), and switch views at any time using the view selector.

| View | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Circles** | Shows every role nested inside its parent. The size of each role depends on the number of sub-roles and members it contains. |
| **Hierarchical tree** | Shows every role as a top-down org chart, each role in a card linked to its parent role, with the members who fill it. |
| **Photo directory** | Shows every member of the organization at a glance, without the roles. |

### Folding a view

The **Folded** option, in the view selector, applies to the **Circles** and **Hierarchical tree** views. It shows only the selected role, its parent roles and their direct roles, all at the same size, instead of the whole organization. On a large organization, this is what keeps the chart comfortable to read.

### Hierarchical tree

The **Hierarchical tree** view presents the same structure as the top-down org chart most organizations are used to. Each role sits in a card linked to its parent role, and members appear inside the card of the role they fill.

Every interaction stays the same as in the circles view: click a role to open it, drag and drop to move a role or a member, zoom and pan freely.

## Sharing (link and embed)

Sharing publishes a public page of your org chart, readable by anyone with the link, with no Rolebase account needed. Organization admins open it from the settings button above the org chart, or from the right click menu on the background, then turn on **Enable sharing**.

Under **Access**, the roles org chart is always part of what you publish, with the names and purposes of the roles only. The **Members** switch adds the members to the public page, with their names and photos only. Everything else stays private.

You then choose the view the public page opens on, whether visitors can zoom and pan, and whether the background stays transparent to blend into your own site. The modal gives you a link to share and an iframe code to embed the org chart in a page of your website.

Turning **Enable sharing** off takes the public page down right away.
