## What is an Organization?

An organization is the **top-level container** in Rolebase. Everything (roles, members, meetings, tasks, discussions, decisions) belongs to an organization. Each organization has its own settings, subscription plan, and member list.

You can be a member of multiple organizations and switch between them from the dashboard.

## Creating an Organization

1. From the **My Organizations** page, click **Create**.
2. Enter a name for your organization.
3. Optionally set a **Custom URL** (e.g. `rolebase.io/my-org`).
4. You are automatically assigned the **Owner** role.

Rolebase then guides you to [choose who makes decisions in your teams, add your main roles and invite the team](/en/docs/getting-started#create-your-organization).

## Organization Settings

Access settings from the organization menu. The following options are available:

### General Settings

- **Name** is the display name of your organization.
- **Slug** is the URL-friendly identifier. Must be unique across Rolebase.

### Governance Settings

The governance mode sets who can change the org chart:

- **Free**: every member can edit the whole org chart.
- **Agile**: roles can be edited by their leaders.
- **Strict**: roles can only be changed through proposals (Holacracy). The org chart stays read-only, and every structure change goes to a vote.

Only the organization Owner can change the governance mode.

See [Governance modes](/en/docs/governance-modes) for a detailed comparison and how to choose the right one.

## Managing Your Organization

### Deleting

Only an Owner can delete an organization, from **Settings > Organization**, in the **Delete the organization** card. Rolebase lists what will be deleted (members, roles, meetings, threads, tasks, decisions, files) and asks you to type the name of the organization to confirm.

The organization is archived right away: its members lose access and its active subscription is cancelled. It is then deleted for good, with all its content, 30 days later. Member accounts are kept.

<Callout type="warning" title="Destructive Action">
  After 30 days, the deletion cannot be undone. Make sure to export any data
  you need before proceeding.
</Callout>

### Deleting your account

You can delete your account from **Settings > My credentials**, in the **Delete my account** card. The deletion is permanent and erases your personal data.

It is possible once you are no longer a member of any organization. The card lists the organizations you still belong to, with your role in each one. [Leave](/en/docs/members) each one from **Settings > Members**, or delete it if you are its Owner. Once your account is deleted, a page confirms it.
