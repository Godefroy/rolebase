A team wants to handle disagreements without letting them escalate. Should it choose a two-day workshop, a programme spread over several months, or a course built for a particular profession? In France, Nonviolent Communication (NVC) training ranges from basic courses led by certified trainers to workplace workshops and specialist programmes. This ranking compares eight options on length, audience, practice, certification, price and funding. Programme details and fees come from providers’ own pages checked on 29 October 2026.

Rolebase publishes this selection. The company makes open-source software for team governance, where organizations record roles, run structured meetings and keep decisions. Rolebase does not provide NVC training. The ranking therefore groups courses by what they teach and who they serve. Links to providers are not affiliate links. Certification pathways suit people who want to build a sustained practice, while short workshops let a team work through workplace situations. Specialist programmes start from the day-to-day context of healthcare or education.

## How this ranking works

The eight courses sit in three groups because a two-day workshop and a 98-hour university diploma do not answer the same need. The first group covers progressive foundation courses, some of which can count towards a certification pathway. The second covers short workplace courses and team programmes. The third focuses on training for a particular profession.

Within each group, the ranking considers four criteria. First, how clearly the provider names its audience and the skills taught. Second, the share of practice, role-play and follow-up. Third, how clearly the provider explains certification and funding. Finally, whether it publishes practical details such as length, fee and the unit the fee covers. Fees come from the providers’ pages. Satisfaction and attendance figures remain attributed to the provider that published them.

The Center for Nonviolent Communication (CNVC) trainer certification and the French professional certificate “Dialoguer avec la CNV pour mieux coopérer” are different qualifications. CNVC certifies trainers who can teach NVC under its certification. The French certificate recognises an initial level of NVC practice in a professional context and does not authorise its holder to teach NVC. Its listing in France’s Répertoire spécifique was stated as valid for three years from 31 May 2024. Check the current status and funding rules before enrolling in 2026.

## 1. La Coop CNV, “Dialoguer avec la CNV pour mieux coopérer”

![La Coop CNV home page](./lacoopcnv.png)

[La Coop CNV](https://lacoopcnv.com/) is the certification body for the professional certificate “Dialoguer avec la CNV pour mieux coopérer.” The complete pathway combines the three foundation modules, or six days, with one remote day for consolidation and preparation. A 30-minute assessment follows. The certificate holder’s published fee is €220 per training day and €200 for the assessment, for a total of €1,740 from module 1. Shorter pathways are available to people who have already completed some of the modules.

The certificate is intended for people working in service of the common good, including in the social and solidarity economy, cooperatives, associations, public services and mission-led companies. The approved training body checks eligibility. To use the CPF, France’s personal training account, the participant must complete the pathway with a single approved body. The certificate recognises an initial level of professional dialogue practice. It does not authorise the holder to teach NVC. Anyone looking to train others should ask CNVC about its trainer certification, which follows a different route.

## 2. Co-Rebond, foundation course in two modules

![Co-Rebond home page](./corebond.png)

[Co-Rebond](https://co-rebond.fr/se-former-a-la-cnv-debuter/) runs a six-day foundation course split into two three-day modules. Its examples and practice can connect NVC principles to work, while the course remains open to the general public. Participants work through observation, clarifying feelings and needs, making requests and practising dialogue.

A page for a 2026 session lists €780 for individuals and €1,200 for freelancers, very small businesses and small associations. Prices for other organizations depend on their size, so the provider asks them to request a quote. Reduced rates may also be available. Co-Rebond says the course may be part of the pathway to “Dialoguer avec la CNV pour mieux coopérer,” if the participant and organization meet its eligibility rules. Dates depend on the session. Check the course page before choosing a schedule.

## 3. ÉlanCvous, NVC foundations for the workplace

![ÉlanCvous home page](./elancvous.png)

[ÉlanCvous](https://elancvous.fr/formation-cnv) presents a three-module foundation course, with two days per module: an introduction to NVC, opening a dialogue and practising dialogue. The trainer states that she is certified by CNVC. The six days take participants from the first steps in the process to conversations during disagreement. The page also lists advanced modules on power, the body, leadership and authenticity in relationships.

Fees vary by audience. A two-day module costs €230 for an individual, €360 for a freelancer or very small business, and €880 for a company. At the published company rate, the full six-day pathway costs €2,640. This lets organizations budget for a longer course, while individuals can take the same foundations at a lower rate. The page does not present this course as the French professional certificate “Dialoguer avec la CNV pour mieux coopérer.” If CPF recognition matters, check the approved pathway and its eligibility rules with the certificate body.

The next three providers offer short workplace formats or courses for teams. Fees apply either per participant or to the group, depending on the format.

## 4. Cercle de la NonViolence, team programmes

![Cercle de la NonViolence home page](./cercle-cnv.png)

The [Cercle de la NonViolence](https://cercle-cnv.com/formations-cnv-intra/) builds courses for healthcare and support professionals. Its 2026 catalogue ranges from a two-hour workshop to a day-long introduction, a two-day foundation module and a five-to-seven-day pathway. A team can begin with an introductory session or practise the foundations over several days. The collective reports 15 trainers in 2026 and more than 1,400 people trained, in professional and personal settings.

For groups of six to sixteen, published 2026–27 rates are €1,400 per day for one to three days and €1,300 per day from five days onwards. The provider says its professional courses are funded by OPCOs, the French sector training bodies. The final quote depends on the format and organization. This option is most relevant to healthcare and support organizations working on situations from their own practice. For a participant who cannot fill a team group, the Cercle says individuals can join its public courses when the group has fewer than six people.

## 5. In Excelsis, workplace NVC practice

![In Excelsis home page](./inxl.png)

[In Excelsis](https://www.inxl.fr/formation/cnv/) runs a two-day in-person course for groups of four to eight. The published inter-company fee is €2,100 excl. VAT per participant. Exercises cover observation, feelings, needs and requests, then listening and difficult workplace conversations. Participants practise with real situations, role-play and filmed exercises. A small group leaves time to repeat a conversation and receive feedback.

The provider says its trainers have completed at least 20 days of NVC training or work as professional NVC mediators. It also states that the course does not certify trainers and does not lead to CNVC trainer certification. The page reports an 80% recommendation rate, while noting that the sample is limited, and a Trustpilot rating of 4.6/5 dated September 2026. OPCO funding depends on the employer’s sector agreement. The published fee is for one inter-company seat. An in-house team course is priced on request.

## 6. Mon Pôle Formation, two-day course

![Mon Pôle Formation home page](./monpoleformation.png)

[Mon Pôle Formation](https://monpoleformation.fr/formation/communication-non-violente-observer-exprimer-ses-besoins-et-demander/) lists a two-day, 14-hour course, either in person or online. Fees start at €1,590 excl. VAT. The programme moves from observing a situation to expressing needs and making a clear request, with exercises and role-play. Inter-company sessions bring people from different organizations together. An in-house session lets a team work through its own situations.

The provider publishes dates through 2027 and reduced rates for several participants booking the same inter-company session. It describes the course as non-certifying and says each participant receives a certificate of attendance. This format suits someone who wants a short, structured workshop without seeking a specific professional certificate. Before booking, compare the selected session’s final price with the starting fee and check the funding rules that apply to your organization.

The final two options serve specific professions. Sorbonne University targets healthcare professionals, while Déclic CNV & Éducation trains people working in education.

## 7. Sorbonne University, university diploma for healthcare professionals

![Sorbonne University continuing education home page](./sorbonne-universite.png)

[Sorbonne University’s continuing education programme](https://fc.sorbonne-universite.fr/nos-offres/du-communication-non-violente-pour-les-professionnels-de-la-sante/) runs a university diploma in NVC for healthcare professionals. The course lasts 98 hours, costs €1,980 and is taught in person at the university’s health faculty. It primarily targets hospital doctors, with places also intended for healthcare managers and administrative managers. The programme addresses care relationships, listening, raising difficult issues, handling tension and cooperation among healthcare teams.

Classes run across several months. The programme gives substantial time to practical workshops, role-play and practice groups, including remote meetings. Participants also write a dissertation and defend it orally. This is a longer commitment than a two-day introductory workshop and follows an academic-year schedule. It is aimed at a specific professional audience, with its own application criteria and possible employer or training-body funding. Confirm the admissions rules and calendar with the university for each cohort.

## 8. Déclic CNV & Éducation, NVC foundations for education

![Déclic CNV & Éducation home page](./declic-cnveducation.png)

[Déclic CNV & Éducation](https://www.declic-cnveducation.org/formation/se-former-aux-bases-de-la-cnv-en-2025-avec-declic/) adapts NVC foundations to educational relationships. Its six-day course is for people working in education and uses situations from their daily work. Sessions announced for 2026–27 cover several cities, including Marseille, Lyon, Vannes, Paris and Bordeaux. Certified CNVC trainers teach the courses.

Déclic holds Qualiopi certification and says funding depends on the participant’s status and institution. The course fee is available by quote, which makes cost comparison less immediate than for providers publishing a price. Participants receive a certificate of attendance. This is not the same as CNVC trainer certification. The course stands out for its focus on education, helping participants relate NVC foundations to situations involving children, young people and education teams.

## An international online option

The [Center for Nonviolent Communication](https://www.cnvc.org/fr/store/nonviolent-communication-online-training-course) also lists an on-demand course built around Marshall Rosenberg’s recorded teaching. The course page describes more than ten hours of material, nine audio sessions and a video Q&A with certified trainer John Kinyon. The course is presented in English, and the page reviewed here does not publish a price. This international option is not part of the France-based ranking above, but it may suit English speakers who want an asynchronous introduction before choosing an in-person programme.

## How to choose an NVC course

Start with what you want to do after the course. A two-day workshop can introduce the process and give you a first chance to practise difficult conversations. In Excelsis and Mon Pôle Formation publish 14-hour formats. Compare group size, exercises, follow-up and the final fee, rather than relying on the words “Nonviolent Communication” in a course title.

If you need a professional qualification, check the exact certificate name and entry rules. “Dialoguer avec la CNV pour mieux coopérer” recognises an initial level of practice and may qualify for CPF funding for eligible participants. CNVC trainer certification follows a different route, designed for people who intend to teach the method. The words “certified” and “certifying” alone do not tell you what the course enables you to do.

For a whole team, ask for an in-house quote that states the number of participants, situations to practise, length and funding arrangements. Cercle de la NonViolence publishes formats for healthcare and support teams. La Coop CNV also builds programmes for organizations. A daily rate may cover the whole group, while an inter-company fee usually applies to each participant.

Choose a specialist course when the work context changes the situations to practise. Sorbonne University targets healthcare professionals. Déclic CNV & Éducation focuses on education. In a team that distributes responsibilities among roles, NVC can help someone express a disagreement or concern. Choose a two-day course for an introduction and first practice, or a six-day pathway if you want to work through the modules over time. ÉlanCvous publishes three two-day foundation modules. The complete “Dialoguer avec la CNV pour mieux coopérer” pathway costs €1,740 at the rate published by its certificate body. Some sessions can count towards the professional certificate “Dialoguer avec la CNV pour mieux coopérer,” subject to the participant’s and organization’s eligibility. Check the certificate’s current status, CPF rules and the specific course before enrolling.

It does not authorise its holder to teach NVC. The qualifications have different purposes and training paths.

Yes. Some courses combine in-person sessions with remote preparation or assessment. Check how much live practice the course includes and how group exercises work remotely.
