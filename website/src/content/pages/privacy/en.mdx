# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-10-26.*

Rolebase (the "Platform"), available at [rolebase.io](https://rolebase.io), is operated by GDFR, a French SASU with a share capital of 1,000 euros, registered office at 61 rue de Lyon, 75012 Paris, France, registered with the Paris Trade and Companies Register under number 102 951 001 (the "Company").

This policy explains which personal data the Company processes, why, for how long, who it shares them with and how to exercise your rights. For any question, write to [contact@rolebase.io](mailto:contact@rolebase.io).

## 1. The Company's two roles

The Company processes personal data in two capacities.

- **Controller.** The Company alone decides why it processes the data related to your account, billing, support, audience measurement, error tracking and its own communications. It answers to you directly for these, under this policy.
- **Processor for your organization.** The content an organization enters in the Platform (members, roles, circles, meetings, minutes, decisions, tasks, threads) belongs to the organization, which is its controller. The Company processes this content only to provide the service and on the organization's instructions, under section 4.2 of the [Terms of Sale](/en/terms). To exercise your rights over this content, contact the organization first. If the Company receives such a request directly, it forwards it to the organization. When an Owner deletes the organization, its content is no longer accessible and is deleted for good 30 days later.

The Company does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising.

## 3. Recipients

Only the Company and its technical providers access the data, to the extent their task requires. These providers are bound to the Company by a contract imposing the obligations of Article 28 of the GDPR. They fall into the following categories:

- hosting of the Platform and its data, in the European Union
- hosting of the public website
- email delivery
- online payment
- accounting
- email and support chat
- audience measurement and error tracking
- artificial intelligence features

Artificial intelligence features send the provider the content needed for the requested task, for example the notes of a meeting to summarize. This provider is contractually bound not to use these data to train its models.

The Company also discloses data when the law or a court order requires it.

## 4. Transfers outside the European Union

Some payment, artificial intelligence, email and website hosting providers process data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified providers, and by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can get a copy of these safeguards by writing to [contact@rolebase.io](mailto:contact@rolebase.io).

## 5. Services you connect

If you connect your Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook calendar, the Platform only stores and transfers the information needed to synchronize your Rolebase meetings. Events that do not come from Rolebase are never retrieved or stored. These synchronizations comply with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy) and the [Microsoft APIs Terms of Use](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/microsoft-apis/terms-of-use).

If you connect an artificial intelligence assistant to the Platform, or join a video call from a meeting link, the data you send to these services fall under their own terms.

## 6. Cookies and trackers

The Platform only uses trackers needed for it to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your display preferences and running the support chat. These trackers are exempt from consent.

Audience measurement works without cookies. It produces statistics for the Company only, which does not combine them with any other source and does not share them with anyone.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. You can object at any time to audience measurement and to product announcements. Every marketing email contains an unsubscribe link.

To exercise these rights, write to [contact@rolebase.io](mailto:contact@rolebase.io) from your account's email address. The Company answers within one month.

Organization administrators can export all of the organization's data from the Platform at any time.

You can delete your account at any time from the Platform settings, once you no longer belong to any organization. Your account data are erased right away. The content you contributed to an organization belongs to it and stays there. If you are the Owner of an organization, you can delete it from its settings: its content is deleted for good 30 days later.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/en/complaints), or with the authority of your country.

## 8. Security

Platform data are hosted in the European Union and backed up regularly. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are stored encrypted, and access to data is restricted by organization and by circle. Only the Company's president has access to production data.

Keep your password confidential and tell the Company immediately if your account is used without your permission.

Avoid entering sensitive data in the Platform (health, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade union membership, ethnic origin, sexual orientation). Users remain responsible for the content they publish.

## 9. Minors

The Platform is reserved for people aged 18 or over with full legal capacity.

## 10. Changes to this policy

The Company may change this policy. In case of a significant change, it informs users through a notification in the Platform or by email.
